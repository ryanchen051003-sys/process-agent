# Meal Expense Policy (fake document for learning)

Applies to DemoCo client meals and overtime meals. This is not a Kingdee or any real-company policy.

## Caps

- Client entertainment: 150 yuan per person per meal, 800 yuan per event. An external guest is required.
- Overtime meal: 40 yuan per person when still on site after 21:00 on a working day, at most 8 times per month.
- Team dinner: once per quarter, 80 yuan per person, with the department head’s pre-approval.

## Rules

- Alcohol is not reimbursable. Tea and soft drinks are allowed and count toward the cap.
- Invoices must show date and restaurant name. The claim must list attendees.
- Do not put overtime meals and client entertainment on the same claim form.
- Finance rejects claims with no attendee list or no invoice.
