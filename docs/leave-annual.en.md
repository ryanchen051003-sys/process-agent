# Annual Leave Policy (fake document for learning)

Applies to full-time DemoCo staff. This is not a Kingdee or any real-company policy.

## Allowance

- 1 year up to 10 years of service: 10 working days per year
- 10 years up to 20 years: 15 working days per year
- 20 years or more: 20 working days per year
- Less than 1 year: prorated by calendar days on payroll; round up to 1 day if under 1 day

## Rules

- Annual leave is counted in working days. Public holidays and company rest days are excluded.
- Submit in the leave system at least 5 working days in advance. It takes effect after the line manager approves.
- One request may not exceed 5 consecutive working days. Longer leave needs a director counter-sign.
- At most 5 unused days may carry to 31 March of the next year. After that they expire.
- On resignation, approved unused annual leave is paid at base salary. Prorated leave under 1 year of service is not paid out.
