# Sick Leave Policy (fake document for learning)

Applies to full-time and part-time DemoCo staff. This is not a Kingdee or any real-company policy.

## Allowance

- After 6 months of service: up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year
- Under 6 months: up to 3 paid sick days per calendar year
- Sick days beyond the paid cap are paid at 60% of base salary and may not exceed 30 calendar days in one stretch

## Rules

- Sick leave of 1 working day or more needs a clinic note within 2 working days after return.
- Three consecutive working days or more need a note from a tertiary hospital or a company-listed clinic.
- Late or missing notes are treated as personal leave and are unpaid.
- Sick leave and annual leave must not be combined on one request form.
