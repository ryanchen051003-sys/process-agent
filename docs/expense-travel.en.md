# Travel Expense Policy (fake document for learning)

Applies to DemoCo business trips. This is not a Kingdee or any real-company policy.

## Eligible items

- Transport: second-class rail / high-speed rail, economy airfare. Business class needs a director’s pre-approval.
- Local transport: metro, bus, and taxis at cost, capped at 200 yuan per day.
- Hotel: 600 yuan per night cap in tier-1 cities, 400 yuan elsewhere.
- Per diem: 80 yuan per person per calendar day when away from the home city for 8 hours or more. Do not claim per diem and a meal invoice for the same day.

## Rules

- A pre-approved trip request is required. IDs start with TR.
- Invoices must be VAT ordinary or e-invoices titled to DemoCo.
- File the claim within 10 working days after the trip. Late claims need a director note.
- If personal travel is mixed in, that segment’s transport and hotel cannot be claimed.
