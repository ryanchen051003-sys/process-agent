# Personal Leave Policy (fake document for learning)

Applies to full-time DemoCo staff. This is not a Kingdee or any real-company policy.

## Allowance

- Up to 8 unpaid working days of personal leave per calendar year.
- One request may not exceed 3 working days.

## Rules

- Submit at least 2 working days in advance. Emergencies may be filed within 1 working day after the fact, with a manager note.
- No base pay, meal stipend, or transit stipend during personal leave.
- After 8 days in a year, further personal leave needs a director and may affect the quarter rating.
- Parental, marriage, and bereavement leave do not use this cap. Those files are not in this week’s folder.
